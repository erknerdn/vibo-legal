Child safety standards

OME is a video chat app for adults aged 18 and over only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

What is prohibited

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited. This includes sharing, requesting or pointing to child sexual abuse material, contacting minors for sexual purposes (grooming), involving minors in live video calls, and any sexual talk about children.

Age limit

We ask for date of birth at sign-up, and people under 18 cannot create an account. On Google Play the app targets adults only, and access is restricted for users Google identifies as minors. Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are closed.

Reporting and blocking

Every user can report and block another person with one tap from the call screen, a chat or a profile. Child safety reports are reviewed with priority. To report from outside the app, email eridenomer@gmail.com.

Enforcement

Accounts that break these standards are permanently banned without warning. When we find child sexual abuse material or behaviour, we preserve the relevant records and report it to the competent authorities as the law requires (in Türkiye, the General Directorate of Security and ihbarweb.org.tr; internationally, NCMEC).

Live video

Video calls are streamed live and are not recorded. Users can end a call at any moment and report the other person.

Child safety point of contact

For any child safety question or report: eridenomer@gmail.com
