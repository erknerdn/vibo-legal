Deleting your Vibo account

In the app: Open Vibo, open your Profile, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete my account and confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

If you can't use the app: Email eridenomer@gmail.com from the address linked to your account with the subject "Delete my account". After confirming it's you, we delete the account within 30 days.

What's deleted: Your profile, photos, device records, coin balance and history, follows, blocks and messages.

What's kept: Records shared with other members (call history, gift records, reports about you) with your link removed, kept for safety and accounting for as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription in Google Play or the App Store; cancel it in the store's subscription settings.
