OME Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the OME mobile app ("OME", "the app"), why we use it and what your rights are. The data controller is ErkanApp ("we"). You can reach us at eridenomer@gmail.com.

OME is only for people aged 18 or over. We delete accounts we learn belong to anyone under 18.

1. Data we collect

Account data. When you sign in with Google or Apple: your email address and the user ID the sign-in provider gives us.

Profile data. Username, date of birth (others only see your age), gender and whether to show it, country, languages, bio, interests and the photos you upload.

Usage data. Invites you send and receive; when your calls start and end, how long they last and the coins spent; who you follow and block; reports you send; when you were last online; and in-app preferences (filters, language).

Device data. Your device's push token so we can send notifications, its operating system and your language.

Purchases. Pro and coin purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details; we keep only what was bought, when, and the transaction ID.

Video calls. Calls are relayed live. We do not record or store their video or audio.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile to others.
  • To run invites, video calls, notifications and messaging.
  • To manage your gift time, coin balance and Pro membership.
  • To enforce the Community Rules: review photos and reports, restrict accounts that break the rules and prevent fraud.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (providing the service), our legitimate interests (safety and preventing abuse), legal obligations and, where needed, your consent (for example for notifications, camera and microphone access).

3. What others can see

Other members can see your username, age, gender (if you chose to show it), country, languages, bio, interests, approved photos and, unless you hide it, whether you're online. Your email and date of birth are never shown. People you block can't see you, and you can't see them.

4. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the service:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • LiveKit: live relay of video calls (no recording).
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your device.
  • Google and Apple: sign-in and in-app purchases.

Their servers may be outside Turkey, for example in the European Union or the United States. These transfers are needed to provide the service and are made with the safeguards the law requires.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We may share it with authorities when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, device records, coin history and everything else that belongs only to you is permanently deleted. In records shared with other members (call history, gift records and reports about you) your link is removed; these records may be kept, no longer tied to you, for safety and accounting for as long as the law requires.

6. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete my account. For anything else, write to eridenomer@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

7. Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules make sure you can only see your own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll announce important changes in the app; the current version is always on this page.
